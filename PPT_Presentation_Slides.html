    Slide 2: Problem Statement & ML Suitability



    Slide Content

  • The Challenge:
    • - Educational institutions rely on reactive end-of-term exams. - At-risk students are identified too late for effective intervention. - Academic outcomes depend on complex, multi-dimensional socio-economic and behavioral factors.
  • Why Machine Learning?:
    • - Non-Linear Dynamics: Captures subtle interactions between study time, parental education, absences, and academic scores. - Scalability: Enables instant evaluation of thousands of student profiles. - Proactive EdTech: Powers real-time early warning alerts for educators and counselors.

    Slide 3: Dataset Architecture & Feature Engineering



    Slide Content

  • Dataset Source: Benchmark UCI Student Performance Dataset (33 total fields).
  • Core Feature Categories:
    • - Academic Records: Period 1 grade ($G_1$), Period 2 grade ($G_2$), past failures (`failures`). - Study Habits: Weekly study time (`studytime`), extra educational support (`schoolsup`), class absences (`absences`). - Socio-Economic & Demographics: Parental education (`Medu`, `Fedu`), family status (`famsize`, `Pstatus`), internet access (`internet`).
  • Data Preprocessing:
    • - `OneHotEncoder`: Encodes 17 categorical variables cleanly. - `StandardScaler`: Normalizes 15 continuous variables to zero mean / unit variance. - `Train-Test Split`: 80% Training ($N=800$) / 20% Evaluation ($N=200$).

    Suggested Diagram / Visual Layout

  • Diagram: Feature Taxonomy Grid / Block Diagram displaying 4 color-coded feature buckets connected to a central Preprocessing Transformer block (`ColumnTransformer`).
